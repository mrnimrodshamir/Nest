NestUp Terms of Use

Effective: September 6, 2026

These Terms govern your use of NestUp, a community service for parents and caregivers to discover public places and events and arrange family activities.

Community safety

You must not post objectionable, abusive, threatening, hateful, sexual, exploitative, deceptive, illegal, or privacy-invasive content. Harassment, bullying, impersonation, spam, scams, and attempts to endanger a child or adult are prohibited.

Your content

You remain responsible for activities, profile information, messages, forum posts, and images you submit. Only share content you have the right to use. Do not publish another person’s private information or precise home location without permission.

Reports, blocking, and moderation

Members can report content or accounts and block other members. NestUp may review reports and remove content, restrict access, suspend, or terminate accounts when reasonably necessary for safety or compliance. Enforcement decisions are reviewed by a person; reports do not trigger automatic punishment.

Activities and external events

User-created activities are arranged by community members, not supervised by NestUp. Public events and places may come from municipalities or other providers. Verify important details with the organiser. A NestUp RSVP is not registration with an external organiser.

Accounts

Provide accurate information, protect your account, and use the service lawfully. Parents and caregivers are responsible for decisions concerning children in their care.

Privacy

Our Privacy Policy explains how data is handled. NestUp does not display another member’s live device location; map pins represent activities, public events, and places.

NestUp Support

Need help with NestUp? Email us at nimrodshamir@nestup.best and we'll get back to you.

Account & login

If you're having trouble signing in, check that you're using the same method you originally signed up with (email and password, or Sign in with Apple). If you signed up with Apple and don't remember creating a password, use "Continue with Apple" rather than "Log in with email."

Forgot your password?

On the login screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your account email. We'll send a reset link — open it on the device with NestUp installed to set a new password. If the link has expired or was already used, request a new one the same way.

Privacy & deletion requests

You can edit or delete your profile, your children's information, and your account at any time from Profile → Settings in the app. To request a copy of your data or ask us to delete it on your behalf, email nimrodshamir@nestup.best. See our Privacy Policy for details on what we collect and why.

Reporting a problem

To report another member, an activity, or a message, use the report option available on that item in the app, or email us with details and we'll follow up.

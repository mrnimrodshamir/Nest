You don't need to build a community. You just need to find the one that's already around you.

What you'll find

  • Parents nearby

    Families a few streets away, with children around the same age. No feeds, no follower counts — just who is actually close.

  • Activities to join

    A playground meetup, a walk, a coffee. Host one in under a minute, or join one already happening this afternoon.

  • Family-friendly places

    Parks, libraries, cafés and indoor playgrounds that genuinely work with a stroller — checked, not crowdsourced guesses.

  • What's on in the city

    Story times, workshops and family events happening around you, filtered down to the ones worth leaving the house for.

  • Local conversations

    Forums for the questions you'd rather ask a neighbour than a search engine — sleep, daycare, where to go on a hot Tuesday.

  • Real people

    Profiles show a first name, a neighbourhood and how old the children are. Never an address, never a phone number.

Built for how parenting actually goes

Most days there isn't a plan. There's a free morning, a child who needs to be somewhere, and no idea who else is around. NestUp is for that morning.

It works for every kind of family — first-time parents, second-time parents who've moved neighbourhoods, mums, dads, grandparents doing the pickup, anyone raising a small person in this city.
